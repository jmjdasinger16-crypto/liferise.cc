Questions before you begin

Frequently Asked Questions

LifeRise is designed to feel approachable from the first conversation.

How much does LifeRise cost?

Membership is $18 every two weeks after the 3-day trial.

Can I cancel?

Yes. Membership can be canceled anytime according to the cancellation terms provided at checkout and in the LifeRise Terms of Service.

Do I need to know exactly what I want to improve?

No. Feeling stuck or dissatisfied is enough of a starting point. Your coach can help you identify the area that would create the most relief or momentum.

Will I be given a strict routine?

No. Your plan is built around your current capacity, responsibilities, schedule, and comfort. The goal is sustainable progress—not perfection.

What areas can LifeRise help with?

Mindset, physical health, nutrition, finances, career, parenting, relationships, social connection, routines, confidence, purpose, and other everyday life challenges.

Is LifeRise therapy or medical care?

No. LifeRise provides lifestyle coaching, education, accountability, and practical support. It does not replace licensed medical, mental-health, legal, or financial professionals.
