About us

A holding company built for the long term.

LifeRise Unlimited Corporation operates and holds businesses, investments, and property with a steady, disciplined approach.

Who we are

LifeRise Unlimited Corporation is a privately held holding company. We operate businesses directly, and we hold a portfolio of investment and real estate positions alongside them. Our approach favors long-term ownership over short-term speculation.

What we operate

Our current operating business is Shedlr, a lead generation platform that supplies local service businesses with manually verified customer leads across a wide range of categories.

What we hold

Beyond our operating business, LifeRise Unlimited Corporation maintains a diversified portfolio of investment holdings and real estate holdings. Details on both are on our Portfolio page.

Our approach

We favor durable businesses and assets over trends, reinvest in what we hold, and take a patient, long-horizon view across everything in our portfolio.
