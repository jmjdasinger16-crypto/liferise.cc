Important information

Coaching scope
& disclaimer.

LifeRise is intended to provide lifestyle coaching, education, encouragement, goal support, and accountability. It is not a substitute for licensed professional services.

Lifestyle coaching

LifeRise coaching may help clients clarify goals, explore options, build routines, improve accountability, and make practical lifestyle changes. Coaching does not guarantee specific personal, health, relationship, career, or financial outcomes.

Medical and mental-health care

LifeRise coaches do not diagnose, treat, cure, or prevent medical or mental-health conditions unless a particular provider is separately licensed and clearly acting within that professional role. Coaching should not replace care from a physician, therapist, counselor, dietitian, or other qualified healthcare professional.

Anyone experiencing a medical emergency, thoughts of self-harm, danger, abuse, or an urgent mental-health crisis should contact emergency services or an appropriate licensed crisis resource immediately.

Financial, legal, and professional advice

General education about budgeting, organization, career development, and decision-making is not individualized financial, investment, tax, accounting, or legal advice. Consult an appropriately licensed professional before making consequential decisions.

Client responsibility

Clients remain responsible for their choices, actions, safety, and results. You should consider your circumstances and seek professional guidance when needed before acting on information discussed through coaching.

Privacy

LifeRise may collect information submitted through the website, including contact details and coaching-intake responses, to provide services, respond to requests, operate the program, and meet legal obligations. Payment information is processed by Stripe and is subject to Stripe’s applicable terms and privacy practices.
