Privacy Policy.

This Privacy Policy explains how LifeRise Unlimited Corporation collects, uses, shares, and protects personal information, including mobile and SMS information.

1. Information We Collect

We may collect information you provide directly to us, including your name, email address, telephone number, business information, inquiry details, service interests, communications, and information submitted through our website, forms, email, telephone calls, or text messages. We may also collect technical information such as IP address, browser type, device information, referring pages, and website usage data.

2. How We Use Information

We use information to respond to inquiries, provide requested services, operate and improve our businesses and websites, communicate with customers and prospective customers, process transactions, provide support, prevent fraud and abuse, comply with legal obligations, and send communications where permitted by law and consent.

3. SMS and Mobile Information

If you provide your mobile number and consent to receive text messages from LifeRise Unlimited Corporation, we may send promotional, informational, consulting, lead-generation, account, support, or service-related SMS messages consistent with the consent you provided.

Mobile information, including phone numbers and SMS opt-in consent data, will not be shared with or sold to third parties or affiliates for their marketing or promotional purposes. We may share information with service providers solely as necessary to operate our messaging program, such as telecommunications or messaging providers, and only for purposes of providing services on our behalf.

4. SMS Choices

Message frequency may vary and will not exceed 4 marketing messages per month unless you separately request or initiate additional service-related communication. Message and data rates may apply. Reply STOP to opt out of SMS messages. Reply HELP for assistance. Consent to receive marketing text messages is not required as a condition of purchase.

5. Information Sharing

We may share personal information with vendors and service providers that help us operate our business, process payments, host websites, provide communications, maintain records, perform analytics, or provide customer support. We may also disclose information when required by law, legal process, or governmental request, or when reasonably necessary to protect rights, safety, property, or security.

6. Cookies and Analytics

Our websites may use cookies, analytics technologies, and similar tools to maintain functionality, understand website traffic, remember preferences, improve performance, and protect against fraud or abuse.

7. Data Retention and Security

We retain information for as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes for which it was collected, to maintain business records, comply with legal obligations, resolve disputes, and enforce agreements. We use reasonable administrative, technical, and organizational safeguards designed to protect personal information, but no method of transmission or storage is completely secure.

8. Your Privacy Choices

You may contact us to request access to, correction of, or deletion of personal information associated with you, subject to applicable law and legitimate retention requirements. We may need to verify your identity before completing a request.

9. Third-Party Services

Our websites may link to or use third-party services. Those services are governed by their own privacy practices and policies.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Material changes will be posted on this page with a revised effective date.

11. Contact

For privacy or SMS-related questions, contact LifeRise Unlimited Corporation at support@liferise.cc or (307) 303-7530.
